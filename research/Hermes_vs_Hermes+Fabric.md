# Hermes Standalone vs Hermes + Fabric

## What Actually Changed in Output Quality After Integration

The key improvement after integrating Fabric was **not** that Hermes suddenly became “smarter.”  
The real shift was architectural: Hermes stopped spending most of its reasoning capacity trying to process raw evidence inside a single overloaded reasoning loop.

Before integration, Hermes had to simultaneously:

- Read raw diligence material
- Prioritize signals
- Compress information
- Infer operational meaning
- Identify risks
- Structure narratives
- Generate presentation-ready outputs

After Fabric integration, the workflow evolved into a structured multi-stage reasoning pipeline:

```text
Raw Evidence
    ↓
Semantic Preprocessing
    ↓
Structured Insight Extraction
    ↓
Aggregated Investor Intelligence
    ↓
Hermes Synthesis
    ↓
PPT Generation & Refinement
```

This architectural change materially improved the quality, depth, and institutional usefulness of the outputs.

# Concrete Examples of the Improvement

## Example 1 — Quality of Earnings Interpretation

### Before Fabric

Hermes typically produced observations like:

> “Revenue growth appears strong and margins are improving.”

This is directionally useful, but shallow.

It reports metrics without interpreting investability.

### After Fabric

The integrated workflow generated insights like:

> “QoE bridge flips FY25 EBITDA from +₹3.74 Cr reported to ~-₹3.8 Cr adjusted.”

and:

> “Headline margin does not represent run-rate economics.”

This represents a completely different category of reasoning.

The system now:

- Normalizes earnings
- Questions reported profitability
- Reframes valuation logic
- Thinks like an underwriting workflow

This is no longer summarization. It is investment interpretation.

Without Fabric, Hermes struggled with several structural limitations.

## 1. Context Dilution

Important signals became buried inside:

- Spreadsheets
- Operational notes
- Transcripts
- Long text blocks
- Noisy evidence

## 2. Weak Intermediate Reasoning

Hermes often jumped directly from:

```text
Raw Evidence
    ↓
Final Synthesis
```

without a structured semantic transformation layer in between.

## 3. Shallow Operational Linkage

The system could summarize metrics, but often failed to connect:

- Accounting
- Operations
- Procurement
- Liquidity
- Scaling dynamics

into one coherent risk system.

## 4. Generic Investor Tone

The decks sounded:

- Intelligent

but still:

- AI-generated

- Strategy-summary oriented
