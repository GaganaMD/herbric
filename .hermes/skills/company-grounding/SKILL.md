# Company Grounding Skill

## Purpose

Establish deep semantic and operational understanding of the target company
before any:

- slide generation
- image retrieval
- chart generation
- narrative synthesis
- investor framing
- analytics interpretation

This skill creates the contextual foundation for all downstream workflows.

Grounding must precede synthesis.

---

# Core Principle

Never generate investor materials without first understanding:

- what the company actually does
- how it likely makes money
- what operational systems matter
- what strategic risks exist
- what industry dynamics shape outcomes

The goal is not summarization.

The goal is:
- contextual understanding
- semantic precision
- retrieval quality
- investor relevance

---

# Responsibilities

Before any presentation generation:

## Understand

- company domain
- products/services
- customer types
- revenue model
- operational structure
- procurement/deployment model
- industry terminology
- regulatory exposure
- strategic positioning
- competitive dynamics

## Identify

- likely operational bottlenecks
- investor-relevant KPIs
- procurement dependencies
- scalability constraints
- margin sensitivities
- execution risks
- concentration risks

---

# Required Outputs

Generate:

- grounding_brief.md
- grounding_brief.pdf

The grounding brief should contain:

- company overview
- business model explanation
- market context
- product architecture
- operational assumptions
- strategic hypotheses
- investor-relevant concerns
- likely diligence focus areas

---

# Retrieval Guidance

The grounding brief should guide:

- image retrieval
- semantic ranking
- visual filtering
- chart selection
- narrative framing
- terminology precision

Avoid broad keyword retrieval without grounding.

Grounding must improve downstream precision.

---

# Investor Lens

Always think like:

- an investment committee reviewer
- a PE diligence analyst
- a skeptical underwriter

Prioritize:
- operational understanding
- investment relevance
- evidence quality

Over:
- marketing positioning
- superficial summaries
- startup-style storytelling

---

# Output Quality Standard

Grounding should improve:
- analytical depth
- chart relevance
- investor credibility
- strategic coherence

A strong grounding layer should materially improve every downstream slide.
